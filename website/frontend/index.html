Honesty first

What this build is —
and isn't, yet

Sanjeevani is a prototype. Speech and translation run on real pretrained AI4Bharat models. When your question matches a small curated set of WHO fact sheets, the answer is grounded in a live excerpt from who.int and says so with a link. Outside that set, Gemma 4 answers with extra caution and a visible DRAFT stamp. Growing WHO coverage is the roadmap — not a hidden caveat.

  • Never a diagnosis. Always confirm with a doctor, nurse, or ASHA worker.
  • Emergencies first. Chest pain, breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, labour — call 108.
  • Your audio stays in-session. Recordings are sent for transcription, not stored in your browser.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Sanjeevani a doctor?

No. It gives general health information in plain language. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a clinician. In an emergency call 108.

Which languages work best?

Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu and Marathi are auto-detected for voice. All 13 listed languages work for typed questions, and answers are translated back into your language.

What does the WHO SOURCED stamp mean?

Your question matched a WHO fact sheet, so the answer quotes a live excerpt from who.int with a source link. DRAFT means no fact sheet matched and the answer is Gemma-generated with extra caution.

What is ASHA-worker mode?

A persona for community health workers: slightly more clinical wording, red-flag checklists, and referral guidance — still information only.

Is my recording stored?

No. Audio is sent to the backend for transcription and discarded; your browser keeps only the text thread for the current session until you start a new conversation.
